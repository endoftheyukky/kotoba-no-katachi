Modifications: the word vectors are not distributed. This table holds, for
the most frequent words and every single kanji, nine numbers per word (its
place on nine axes between two sets of anchor words), each quantised to one
of 64 levels. How they were made: tools/semantic/axes.py and shard.py in the
work's repository, and meta.json in this directory.
